About Ingabo Kreatives

A photography and film agency built on one idea: the moments and stories that matter deserve to be told well.

Who we are

Ingabo Kreatives is a creative production agency working in photography and videography, based in Kigali, Rwanda.

We work with individuals, couples and families, with businesses and brands, and with organisations, NGOs and event organisers who want visuals with substance as well as style. Some clients come to us for a single portrait session; others for a wedding, a conference or a full brand film.

Whatever the project, we bring the same care: we listen first, plan properly and treat every frame as part of a larger story.

We don’t only record what happened. We look for what it meant, and make images that still feel true years from now.

What guides our work

Four principles behind every photograph and film we make.

Visual storytelling

We think in sequences, not single shots: a beginning, a feeling, the details and the people that make it yours.

Authenticity

Real expressions and real moments. We direct when it helps and step back when it matters.

Craft and quality

Considered light, composition and editing, with the same standard for a small session as for a large production.

Partnership

Clear communication from the first message to the final delivery. You always know what’s happening and what comes next.

How we tell a story

We listen before we shoot

Every project begins with a conversation about who you are, what you need and how the images will be used. That shapes everything that follows.

We plan for the moments that matter

Timelines, locations, light and logistics are worked out in advance, so on the day we can focus completely on what’s happening in front of the lens.

We observe, then we direct

The best images often happen between the planned ones. We stay alert to them, and guide gently when a moment needs shaping.

We finish with care

Editing is where a story takes its final form. We select, grade and refine every image and film so the whole set feels consistent and complete.

How we work with you

A simple, transparent process for every project, large or small.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We get to know your vision, your goals and what you expect from the final images or film.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Together we agree the creative direction, schedule, locations and everything the production needs.

  3. 03

    Create

    On the day, we capture and produce the visual content, calmly and with full attention.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    We edit and finalise your photographs and films, then deliver them ready to share.
